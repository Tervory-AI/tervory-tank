# zenas.deadlines

## 1.1.0
- Court-day counts no longer skip 16 December to 15 January unless the rule says so. Uniform Rule 19(1) (notice of intention to defend, 10 court days) and Uniform Rule 26 (five court days after a notice of bar) do. A plea under Uniform Rule 22(1) and a generic court-day count do not.
- A claim against the State is 20 court days under Uniform Rule 19(2). That subrule does not contain the festive exclusion, so those weekdays are counted. The panel should confirm this reading.
- A criminal request is refused. No criminal time period is calculated.
- One court day now reads "runs".
- 1.0.0 is unchanged.

## 1.0.0
- First version. Pure code. FRCP 6 for day and hour periods, including federal legal holidays and an optional state-holiday list. Uniform Rules 1 and 19 for South African court days, public holidays, and the 16 December to 15 January exclusion on every South African count. Each step cites the rule it applied.
