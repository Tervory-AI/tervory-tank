# Client sidebar — direct founder specification

Source: direct founder message, 30 October 2026. This replaces previous client sidebar structures. It does not approve the previously rejected page contents.

## Top — standard on every product and page

1. Client logo; leave blank if the client has not uploaded one.
2. Client account name.
3. Dashboard
4. Projects
5. Chats
6. Integrations
7. Connections

Integrations opens this submenu:

- PlugIns
- MCP

Connections is a separate top-menu item, at the same level as Integrations. It opens:

- SSH for external Server
- Connect External Computer
- Connect to the Cloud

The founder explicitly corrected the earlier nested Connect proposal: use Connections, not Connect, and never nest it under Integrations.

These are founder-specified navigation labels. The menu instruction does not authorise creating connections, reading credentials, changing servers or inventing connection-page copy.

## Middle

The first two items are standard everywhere:

1. The Squad
2. New Notices

The Squad opens a submenu containing all products from the actual product catalogue. It is distinct from the chosen-product shortcuts below. Listing a product does not grant access or change server authorisation. This updates the earlier Squad label rather than creating a duplicate row.

Below these, list the products the client has chosen. Each opens its own product window. Do not infer that this requires a new browser tab.

The remaining product-specific navigation must suit that product: Base's middle menu must differ from Tiger's. This specification does not invent those product-specific items; use their approved product requirements and real client entitlements.

## Bottom — standard everywhere

1. Tervory AI logo
2. Settings
3. Support
4. Contact Us

## Boundaries

- The top, bottom and first two middle items are standard across products and pages.
- A missing client logo stays blank; do not add an invented placeholder or upload control.
- Account identity and chosen-product visibility come from real client data.
- Earlier global menus are superseded; do not silently retain their extra items.
- Preserve existing authorisation and tenant isolation. Navigation visibility does not replace server authorisation.
- Apply this structure in Figma first, including desktop and mobile, then verify exact labels, order and behaviour against this direct specification.
- This menu specification does not approve the rejected client dashboard, signup, billing, savings or developer-oriented window content.
- Rand is an option only for Zenas; no global platform currency or residency claim is authorised.
- The AmmoSync case-study experience is client-facing results, not briefs, PRs, GitHub or merge instructions.

## Founder wording

> Top and Bottom Menu and the two first items in the middle menue are standardised.
