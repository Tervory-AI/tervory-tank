## Wording and sources

Every visible string in [checks and copy inventory](evidence/final-checks.json) is conservatively **Proposed**, except the founder-supplied Street Law scope and volunteer-help direction. Existing product text includes “Zenas provides legal information, not legal advice.” and the checking-law loading line (`workspace/app/zenas/view.tsx:404`, `:478`). Buttons, headings, queue labels, privacy/share explanation and example question/answer are Proposed. All pages carry the Proposed notice.

The rental-deposit example is limited to requesting written proof of interest during the lease. It illustrates a sourced answer; it is not a real client answer or a legal outcome promise. Citation [1]: [Rental Housing Act 50 of 1999, section 5(3)(d)](https://lawlibrary.org.za/akn/za/act/1999/50/eng%402008-05-13). Additional source: [Western Cape Government tenant questions](https://www.westerncape.gov.za/infrastructure/frequently-asked-questions-tenants). Both were checked on 4 Nov. The prototype source buttons open those exact addresses. No deadlines or uncited case authorities are invented.

Ask a volunteer explicitly shares the current question/answer in the prototype. The hand-over confirms queue placement, without promising a response time. The Error state explicitly says the question was not sent. Volunteer examples show no contact details.
