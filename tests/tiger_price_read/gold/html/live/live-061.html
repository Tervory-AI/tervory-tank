Delivery, collection & returns

Collection

Collect from store

FREE Collection in store* to your chosen participating UK store. (Exclusions apply)

Choose your collection date at checkout when placing your order. We recommend that the delivery date you select is 24 hours before your special occasion. Food to Order can only be collected in store. Food orders are not able to be delivered to UK or international home addresses.

(More about collecting food orders)

We’ll carefully prepare your food order just for you, so please consider our preparation times when placing your order. Some food orders take longer to prepare than others – take a look at each product to find out when it’ll be ready to collect. Please note that orders can only be amended within 30 minutes of being placed.

(More about preparation times)

Returns

UK returns

If you’d like to speak to us about your food order call us on 0333 014 8222, our lines are open 8am to 7pm every day.

It’s really important that you collect your food order from store on the arranged date. As food orders are perishable, we don’t keep them until the next day, if you don’t collect it on your chosen date we won’t be able to refund you.

Exclusions apply
