Air Jordan 5 Retro "Halloween"

The spooky season's upon us. Step into the shadows in this sinister take on the AJ5. Premium black nubuck cloaks the upper to keep you out of sight. Spine-chilling Picante Red accents emerge like a jack-o-lantern's flickering glare in the dead of night. Glow-in-the-dark details bring a ghostly glimmer to the legendary silhouette. The net-inspired tongue and quarter panels combine with a jagged midsole, bump-out collar and lace locks to revive the original 1990 build. An Air-cushioned heel and forefoot let you ditch the ghosts, ghouls and goblins in comfort, but beware the Black Cat.

Benefits

  • Premium leather and nubuck upper adds durable comfort.
  • Nike Air technology absorbs impact for cushioning with every step.
  • Plush and comfortable, Max Air cushioning has just the right amount of support.
  • Grippy rubber outsole helps provide durable traction.
