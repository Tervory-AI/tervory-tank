## The job, step by step, as a client does it

Intended client journey; the client's own signed-in session has not been verified here:

1. Open `/market-lab` in the client's workspace and choose **Refresh feeds**.
2. Read **On**, which counts feeds whose latest collection stored observations. Missing named keys stay **Off, needs key**; blocked sites stay **Off, blocked**.
3. Ask a question about a stored feed. The local proof asks for California population and active listings in Alameda.
4. Open every linked statement. Its stored document shows the same observation, place, reporting period, value and unit. Revisions are explicitly labelled.

## What changed

- **Production corridor:** `tank/market_lab_routes.py:19` uses Tank's actual `_http` client, following the existing proxy pattern. The nonexistent `shared_http()` call and all Market Lab stand-in Tank fixtures are removed. `tests/test_market_lab_real_tank.py:22` and `agents/market_lab/fixture_proof/server.py:91` exercise the real application.

Freight figures explain their baseline. Bill and regulation titles explain the notice; original wording stays behind a labelled disclosure. Source pages retain publisher attribution and required notices without displaying internal licence-review notes.
- **Relevant answers:** `core/evidence.py:156` constrains named counties, lets the engine identify notice topics, matches displayed names and notice text, and keeps the strongest matches. A question about a bill does not return unrelated checks or temperature; an Alameda question does not return other counties.
- **Page states:** `workspace/app/market-lab/load.ts:12` passes actual stored linked-page checks to `model.ts:151`; `view.tsx:785` omits empty search headings and contradictory empty-state wording. Provider, navigation, saved-link and access-key descriptions now describe the actual controls.

[The audit](evidence/owner-read.md) lists its findings, resolutions, exact coverage and limits. It found no remaining owner-text defect in that inspected scope; it is not the founder's new review.

**Decisions I made**

- Test Tank itself with its normal startup, authentication and HTTP client. Use real room routes and local Postgres. Seed a disposable test database rather than inject replacement Tank interfaces. No fixture replaces a Tank method, client, dependency or application.
- Keep the room worker stopped in the local harness and prove refresh through the persisted queue entry. Grok owns live collection; Claude owns signed-in proof after deploy. Neither is claimed here.
- Retain raw source fields and immutable citation anchors. Add display labels and keep required attribution. Correct the commodity fixture's unit at ingestion rather than relabel a price as an index.
- Keep licence-type/product meanings and notice-topic choices in engine configuration. Replace meaningless background-check fixture codes with named permit categories; do not borrow licence meanings for checks.
- Use named-county filtering and strongest matching facts so a short brief does not mix unrelated places or measurements. No model or paid provider is involved.
- Preserve the founder's public-page rule: one truthful Tervory identity, per-host pacing, stop and record blocks, skip missing named keys, no login/paywall/captcha access.
- Keep one draft PR, do not retry Actions, and report local evidence with its limits.
